    Drag ⠿ (or use ↑ ↓) to change the order. To group places into one tab you swipe through (e.g. San Diego: Home, Downtown), use a place’s Move to… menu and pick New tab…, then move other places into it. Tip: press and hold a tab to jump here.

      GitHub access

      1. Open GitHub’s token page (name and permission are filled in).
      2. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories → weather.
      3. Tap Generate token, copy it, and paste it here.

      Stays on this device only. It can save your synced locations and run this app’s workflows in the weather repo, and nothing else.

      About

      Data: National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for U.S. forecasts, observations, alerts and forecast discussions; Open-Meteo for international forecasts, UV and air quality; NOAA Tides & Currents; Iowa Environmental Mesonet for U.S. radar and HRRR future radar; RainViewer for international radar. Plain-English discussion summaries are written by Claude on a schedule and may lag the newest NWS issuance.
